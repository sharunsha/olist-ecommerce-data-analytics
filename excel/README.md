# Excel Analytics – Olist E-commerce Project

## Overview

This folder contains the Excel analytics workbook developed using the Brazilian Olist E-commerce dataset.

The workbook was designed to organize, analyze, and visualize key business metrics across revenue, products, delivery operations, customer satisfaction, customer segmentation, and seller performance.

## Workbook

**File:** `Olist_Excel_Analytics.xlsx`

## Workbook Structure

The workbook contains 12 analytical sheets:

1. Orders
2. Order_Items
3. Products
4. Customers
5. Payments
6. Reviews
7. Monthly_Revenue
8. Category_Performance
9. Delivery_Monthly
10. Delivery_Satisfaction
11. Customer_Segments
12. Seller_Performance

## Analysis and Visualizations

The workbook includes 11 charts:

### Revenue Analysis

### Product Analysis
- Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue

### Delivery Analysis
- Monthly Average Delivery Time

### Customer Analysis
- Customer Distribution by Segment
- Revenue Contribution by Customer Segment

### Seller Analysis

- Top 10 Sellers by Order Volume
