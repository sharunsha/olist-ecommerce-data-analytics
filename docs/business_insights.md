Olist E-commerce – Consolidated Business Insights
1. Project Overview
This report summarizes the business insights obtained from analyzing the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset using Python, MySQL, Excel and Power BI.
2. Revenue and Business Performance
•	Product revenue: ₹13,591,643.70
•	Freight value: ₹2,251,909.54
•	Total order value: ₹15,843,553.24
•	Unique orders: 99,441
•	Highest monthly revenue: November 2017 (₹1,010,271.37)
Insight: November 2017 recorded the highest monthly product revenue, with a 52.10% increase over October 2017.
3. Product and Category Performance
•	Total product categories: 73
•	Highest revenue category: Health & Beauty
•	Second-highest revenue category: Watches & Gifts
•	Top 10 categories contributed approximately 62.36% of product revenue.
Insight: Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of product categories.
4. Delivery and Logistics
Monthly delivery analysis:
•	Valid delivered orders: 96,470
•	On-time orders: 88,644
•	Late orders: 7,826
•	On-time rate: 91.89%
•	Late rate: 8.11%
Insight: March 2018 recorded the highest late-delivery rate at 21.36%.
Data limitation: Delivery classifications and populations differed between the monthly delivery, MySQL view and delivery satisfaction analyses. The figures are retained separately and have not been reconciled.
5. Customer Satisfaction
Delivery Status	Average Review Score
On Time	4.29 / 5
Late	2.57 / 5
Insight: Late deliveries were associated with substantially lower customer review scores.
6. Customer Segmentation
•	Total segmented customers: 96,096
•	Largest segment: Needs Re-engagement
•	Highest average customer value: Champions (₹276.37)
•	At Risk and Need Attention segments contributed approximately 42.96% of revenue.
Insight: Customer retention and reactivation represent important opportunities.
7. Seller Performance
•	Total sellers: 3,095
•	Seller-order associations: 100,010
•	Items sold: 112,650
•	Weighted late-delivery rate: 8.02%
•	Weighted average review score: 4.09 / 5
Insight: Seller performance varies across revenue, delivery reliability and customer satisfaction.
8. Business Recommendations
1.	Strengthen high-performing product categories.
2.	Improve delivery reliability.
3.	Focus on customer retention and reactivation.
4.	Monitor seller performance and customer feedback.
5.	Improve product-category mapping and data quality.
9. Conclusion
The project demonstrates how data analytics can support e-commerce business decisions by combining revenue analysis, product performance, delivery operations, customer satisfaction, customer segmentation and seller evaluation.
The findings provide actionable opportunities to improve operational efficiency, customer experience and business performance.
